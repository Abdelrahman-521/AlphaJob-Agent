## A. Application stats (updated Oct 8, late evening)
**Totals:** 153 applications in 11 days (Sep 28 to Oct 8). 72 NL employers, 81 national. Routes (from the tracker's route column): about 102 company site, 21 Indeed, 20 email (most of them Job Bank direct-email on Oct 8), 9 LinkedIn Easy Apply, 1 Job Bank. 14 EDGE (⚡). Keyword coverage is logged from #43 on: average about 86%.

**Outcomes so far:** 17 rejections (11%). NL 9 of 72 rejected, national 8 of 81. 1 vacancy cancelled. 2 recorded interviews done Oct 8 (#58, #87). 0 human interviews. Warm contacts reply within a day; cold recruiter outreach (37 people) has 0 replies that moved an application forward.

**What the numbers say (small, so careful):**
- Easy Apply is the worst route by far. Keep it last.
- Rejections don't track keyword coverage (the three with a score were 100%, 93%, 75%): the misses were fit and location, not wording. One wanted Halifax residents; two gave no reason.
- The national batch is under a week old. Judge it on Oct 14, not before.
- Referrals and warm contacts are the only thing producing replies. Every NL application should get a WARM_CHECK and, where it fits, a FACE_VISIT.

**Decision rules:** change a rule only when a group has at least 8 applications and the gap is at least 2 responses. One change at a time, dated in the review log. A clear human signal beats thin stats; act on it right away. Never improve by stretching the truth.

**Starting beliefs (until our data says otherwise):** apply within 48 hours of posting · a referral or warm contact is the strongest lever · 70%+ keyword coverage every time · the right resume variant beats the complete one · company site beats aggregators · one role per company.
