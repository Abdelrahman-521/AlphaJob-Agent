## Skipped and why
The private copy logs every skip with its reason so the agent never re-checks it. The reasons repeat: citizenship or security clearance required, French required, 2 to 5+ years required, a named platform he hasn't used, a licence required at start, temporary or part-time, commission-only, a non-PR occupation code (TEER 4 or 5), or the company is already capped.
